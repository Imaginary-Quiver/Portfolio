The Project

Media/Narrative_Projects/Set 3 Fighting Game Barks.pdf
This was a project I worked on for for my Interactive Digital Narrative Class. The goal was to create a set of barks for a new character in an existing fighting game franchise. After deciding that I wanted to do something for the Injustice franchise, I immediatley thought of my favorite DC joke character, Calendar Man.
.hs
The Process
.txts
Starting with the character's description, I planned out how he would fit into the wider Injustice story. Then I did some reaserch on his first appearances in comics, and looked for common speach patterns so that I could match his dialogue to his past appearances. Through this process I found an easter egg he appears in and decided to pay homage to it and a few other story lines hes in. This process has led to the creation of what I find to be an acurate and witty rendition of Calendar Man as a playable character in Injustice.
